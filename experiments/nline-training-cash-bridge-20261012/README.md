# nLine first-job route: applicant preparation and cash-ambiguity gate

Checked independently against the [employer's current Field Surveyor vacancy](https://nline-inc.breezy.hr/p/f933afb58414-field-surveyor) on 2026-10-12. This project is **not affiliated with nLine**, has not contacted any applicant or employer in this action, and has no verified project-attributable human earnings.

The actual public deliverable in this branch is [APPLICANT_GUIDE.md](APPLICANT_GUIDE.md), a no-registration, privacy-preserving preparation guide. It gives the published town/phone/language conditions, truthful CV and cover-letter guidance, the exact unresolved training-venue/Freetown reimbursement discrepancy, and six facts to check before paying for travel. It directs applicants to the employer's own website and specifically refuses to assume employment, advance cash, or reimbursement.

**Cash sensitivity result (local read-only independent test, not integrated source):** illustrative outbound travel 400 NLe, 350 NLe hypothetical spending on each of five training days, return 400 NLe, and 800 NLe hypothetical late bus reimbursement produce peak starting cash requirements of 400, 400, and 1700 NLe under three invented stipend schedules. End cash is 500 NLe in each case. These are not measured worker costs or employer-confirmed payment schedules. The local original Python/JS source ZIP and full tests have not been uploaded; do not claim they are present in GitHub. The full locally run suite returned 18 Python tests, 62 JavaScript assertions, and 300 seeded parity cases, but these test receipts alone are **not** independently rerunnable from this branch.

**Why this matters:** The training location is likely outside Freetown, whereas the employer's bus-reimbursement wording specifies a trip to Freetown; the actual cash needed depends on the true venue, covered route, and disbursement sequence. The project has no basis to prescribe borrowing, paying an intermediary, buying equipment, or traveling speculatively.

**ACTIVE evidence gate:** volunteer-only, consented, eligible applicant independently checks the employer's actual route, reimbursement, and payout terms; chooses whether to use the public guide and apply directly; provides a non-identifying completion receipt only if they wish. To establish project impact later, obtain independently verifiable incremental accepted work and positive spendable net earnings relative to a credible counterfactual, subtracting all worker and sponsor costs. **Baseline: zero verified assisted applicants, hires and attributable human earnings.**

**WATCHING:** applicant deadline 2026-10-23; training town and date, first-day per-diem amount, reimbursed bus legs, actual costs, fieldwork selection and payment. **Failure:** vacancy closes, no eligible/consenting participant, costs or payment cannot be verified, or no credible incrementality. Then retire the employer-specific path rather than manufacture demand or arrange debt. **Fallback:** qualify another current independently paid real job with a safe direct-to-worker payout, not another unverified survey marketing claim.

**Operational restriction:** no recruiting, contacting nLine, spending, lending, guarantees, collecting personal data, financial transactions or new scheduler is authorized by this guide. GitHub source integration of the local ZIP remains separate work requiring exact bytes and normal review.
