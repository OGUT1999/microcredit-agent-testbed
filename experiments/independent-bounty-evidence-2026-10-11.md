## Low-pay sensitivity (illustration, not estimated probabilities)

A $50 task at 10 hours has $5/hour gross **only if paid with certainty**. If a worker invested 10 hours and an illustrative 50% of attempts paid, expected gross would be **$2.50/hour**; at 20 hours it would be **$1.25/hour**, *before* transfer fees, devices, connectivity, taxes, and onboarding. Actual acceptance odds, hours, eligibility, and net earnings are **unknown**. Low headline bounties therefore cannot be treated as poverty-reducing without measuring real participation economics and displaced alternatives.
