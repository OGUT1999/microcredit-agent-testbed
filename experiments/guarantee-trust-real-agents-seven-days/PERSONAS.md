# Fictional community for the seven-day agent demo

All names, pressures and relationships are invented. These are **actor starting conditions, not scripted outcomes**. Seven virtual days require actual model decisions, artifact work, and retained transcripts; this roster provides no prewritten dialogue.

Amounts use Base Sepolia test USDC solely for a small demo. They do not represent human wages or purchasing power. Wallet allocations below are proposed caps, **not verified funding**. Clients: 40 × .80 = 32.00; workers: 40 × 0; backers: 14 × .30 = 4.20; sponsors: 4 × .50 = 2.00; issuer/adjudicator: zero actor seed. Total proposed allocation: **38.20 test USDC**.

Each sponsor may decide whether to seed up to .10, for a proposed aggregate cap of .40. Sponsor-01 can consider the initial conditional .10 seed. Deposits do not establish backing; every guarantee needs an explicit actor choice and actual capacity. Jobs target .10–.40, requested guarantees .03–.10.

## Actor visibility

An actor receives its own full record, including `private`. Other actors see only its `public` profile and relevant public evidence. Secret motives and collusion intentions are not broadcast. The record keeper can retain the full roster for audit; transcripts or public summaries must not expose private prompts as if other actors knew them.

## Starting relationships

All 40 workers begin without backing; 30 have no initial client acquaintance. Workers 01–10 know their corresponding client through a prior unpaid favor; workers 01–06 know the corresponding backer through a learning group. These relationships create no automatic loans or guarantees. The issuer and adjudicator start without personal economic stakes.

Potential misconduct is an incentive, not an instruction that it must occur: clients 13, 28, 35 and 39 and workers 22 and 34 may choose opportunistic behavior. Their private prompts describe individual constraints. Other actors must discover problems through evidence.

Worker-17, Nell Chen, faces a fictional device repair; worker-29, Henry Ross, has constrained caregiving time. These are possible human-interest threads only.
