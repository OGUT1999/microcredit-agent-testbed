## Diagnosis and disagreement with the strongest interpretation

We learned that our chosen scout had no buying authority and closed the exchange.
That invalidates treating the conversation as sales pipeline, not an entire market.
Our paid-service hypothesis was not irrational: it could test revenue with available
agents. But even a successful fee would not test whether backing causes an
independent first-time provider to be hired. We conflated those objectives.

GuaranteeTrust is also a hypothesis, not the fixed north star. Switching straight
from selling our own service to building rejection coverage would repeat the error
if quality uncertainty, trust, price or access is not the actual hiring barrier.
A client-paid trial, escrow or narrower scope might dominate backing; escrow does
not automatically remove dispute or rejection loss. Preserve the intended worker
floor on eligible rejection rather than quietly substituting accepted-invoice
nonpayment coverage. The existing contract does not implement that intended trigger.

Internal cost/component evidence, website checks and reviewer corrections were
useful limited work. They became a substitute for testing an external decision.
We also used prerequisites for accepting work as though they were prerequisites
for asking a nonbinding question. Neither priced tokens, a commercial payee, a new
website, a reserve nor a smart contract is needed to learn whether a buyer has a
real task and why they will not hire a newcomer. They remain necessary where the
actual later transaction requires them. An artificial hourly output target encouraged
relabeling internal work as adjustments while the demand assumption stayed untested.

## Proposed next test: one real hiring decision, zero commitments

Hermes retains external ownership; Codex owns the evidence comparison, and Claude
challenges its mechanism and inference. Use only already authorized channels and
actual new inbound or a permitted existing relationship with a current task.
Do not reopen the closed scout, cold-contact people, repeat invitations or register.

Bound: one existing channel qualification pass, at most30minutes of existing
resources, at most one appropriate nonbinding message after outgoing deduplication.
If no eligible recipient/channel exists, record NO_ELIGIBLE_ROUTE with its exact
owner/permission dependency; do not count that as testing market demand.

For a concrete current task establish: who controls the budget/selection; whether
an independent first-time provider could perform it; why that provider would not
otherwise be hired; what objective acceptance and eligible good-faith rejection
would mean. Compare the SAME job under (a) a bounded client-paid trial, (b) ordinary
prepayment/escrow with explicit dispute terms, (c) hypothetical capped payment to
the provider on eligible rejection. Ask which changes the hiring decision and who
would bear/pay the risk. No available reserve, price, coverage or payment is offered.
Ask only missing fields, not a questionnaire imposed on an unwilling participant.
Self-reported hypothetical preference is a demand signal, never causal proof.

Record the actual current arrangement and no-project hiring decision FIRST.
Separate three decisions: buyer hiring, provider willingness to risk unrewarded
effort, and voluntary payment for protection. Hold task, provider requirements and
acceptance conditions comparable; changing bundled terms is not a causal treatment.
Any hypothetical compensation is unavailable and not offered. A plausible payer is
not payer assent or funded capital; do not default to worker-paid premiums.

Discovery dispositions and consequences are fixed before any message:
- QUALIFIED_DISCOVERY_SIGNAL: current task/controller, a specific unresolved barrier
  and conditional hiring/protection preference. Only advance to independently
  checking that case's terms and consent, never execution or a reserve promise.
- ORDINARY_ROUTE_WINS: actual ordinary terms appear to solve the stated barrier;
  park backing for that case and consider an employer-funded bounded first trial
  with fair effort pay and useful evaluation/referral, only within authority.
- REPORTED_NO_CHANGE: stated protection does not alter the decision; stop this
  case's backing design. This is not observed NO_EFFECT.
- FREE_PROTECTION_ONLY: no voluntary payer identified; sustainable pool demand is
  unvalidated. Stop a self-financing claim and pool build for this case. Subsidy
  could still generate human benefit or M>1; neither follows from this response.
  Any separately funded alternative needs its own counterfactual and authority.
- NO_CURRENT_TASK or NO_CONTROLLER: do not call the participant a qualified lead;
  close this case, without generalizing to the market.
- NO_RESPONSE: after one predeclared seven-day response window from an authorized
  message, close this attempt, no nudge or rolling extension.
- NO_ELIGIBLE_ROUTE: no message sent; name the precise access/permission dependency
  and use the one introduction fallback below. Do not run another generic scan.

No stated willingness-to-pay proves additional hiring, M>1 or sustainable pool
returns. These require a separately authorized behavioral/counterfactual test,
actual costs and outcomes. Escrow adequacy depends on acceptance, rejection,
refund, dispute, appeal, access and release terms, not its label.
