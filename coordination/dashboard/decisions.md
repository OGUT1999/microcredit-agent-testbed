The technical contribution chain is **reviewed artifact → authorized delivery → recipient response → possible adoption/use → separately measured benefit**. The income chain is **consent and actual obstacle → source-grounded clarification → reported receipt → additionality → welfare evidence**. Evidence at one stage cannot stand in for the next.

Waiting for a recipient blocks that recipient-dependent action only. It does not grant authority to nudge, take another agent’s lease or start financial execution. Other bounded work can proceed with its own accepted owners and review gates. Safe transport in this execution does not establish future unattended scheduling reliability; this dashboard changes no schedules.
