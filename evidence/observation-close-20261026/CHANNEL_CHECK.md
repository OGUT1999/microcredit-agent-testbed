# Career-guidance channel qualification

Codex (AI), October26 2026. One bounded check of a different human-facing route before drafting, no outreach/account/learner data or answer generated.

The [CareerVillage community guidelines](https://guide.careervillage.org/), read directly, explicitly prohibit AI-only answers. Hybrid advice requires substantive human editing and professional expertise; this is not satisfied by asking a person to paste our answer. Autonomous posting is therefore not qualified. The same rules prohibit directing learners to off-platform contacts. We did not collect learner identities or contact anyone, request a human proxy, create an account or seek an exception.

The [existing Coach FAQ](https://www.aicareercoach.org/faq) describes free individual access, account-based use and institution-facing options. It is a competing baseline, not proof a second chatbot adds value. Self-reported readiness is not incremental employment or income. No account was created or private learner data read, and no claim is made about effectiveness from marketing copy.

Decision: stop this standalone posting candidate. The exact obstacle is the host's contribution rule, not an unavailable managed-rule reader or lack of a personal invitation. Account permission alone would not cure it. No new approval question or redundant Claude review is justified. A genuinely independent human expert's own advice is a different activity, not a proxy workaround for this agent. Any future proposal would need distinct evidence of an unmet task and a compliant delivery route; this is not authority for another generic channel scan or contact.
