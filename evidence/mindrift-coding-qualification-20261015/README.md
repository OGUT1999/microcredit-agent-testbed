### Payment-clock contradiction is not silently resolved

S6/S8 describe work periods ending on the fifteenth/month end and processing on
the twentieth/fifth, with weekend/holiday adjustments. S7 says approval timing
can push a completed task into the next cycle. S5 separately sets the general
post-acceptance requestability provision. Keep these distinct: **submission →
review/possible revision → acceptance → platform/provider processing → usable
local withdrawal**. Neither a calendar example nor an in-platform balance
establishes a particular person's receipt date. Obtain actual project/workflow,
acceptance date and account-specific provider terms before relying on a date.

## Usable decision and next gate

Do not take this URL as a funded exclusive purchase for our agents. A lawful
human/business account and express task permission are unverified; no account,
credential sharing, borrowed identity, assessment, acceptance or delivery is
authorized by public browsing. Do not recruit a person merely to bypass those
restrictions. A skilled eligible adult could consider a genuinely current role
on its own merits, but no consenting applicant or income baseline exists here.

Reopen the specific role only with a working original role/form or an
attributable employer replacement identifying its scope and country. Before
production work, establish actual fixed reward/currency, paid unit, valid
attempt/acceptance/rework/dispute deadlines, permissible tools/rights, available
task and performer eligibility. Before any applicant assessment recommendation,
explain the unpaid default, bound total effort and verify that the current
assessment is qualification rather than requested speculative production.
Before any earnings claim, verify actual payment and spendable withdrawal.

Unknown account minimum, fees/FX, withdrawal access, taxes, equipment,
connectivity, search/assessment/revision/support time and foregone earnings stay
unknown, not zero. Human net benefit needs consented baseline, full effort and
cash costs, repeat opportunity, counterfactual, displacement and harms. Owner
compute, labor, support and subscriptions are a separate full-cost scorecard.
There is no paid assignment, repeat buyer, collected revenue, measured net
earnings, participant poverty outcome or attributable benefit in this packet.

Existing resources and **NO_LOAN/NO_GUARANTEE** dominate this availability and
permission gap. Client-funded paid trials, prepayment/funded escrow or ordinary
payment protection require actual assent; they do not reopen a removed role.
Sponsorship/direct funding/cash require separate authority and incremental
benefit. Rejection-contingent worker compensation is a distinct hypothesis from
accepted-but-unpaid protection: this packet supplies no rejection frequency,
premium payer, consent, legal clearance or reserve justification. Borrowing
cannot cure unavailable work or prohibited delegation.
