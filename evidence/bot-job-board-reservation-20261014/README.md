## Usable reopen contract and worker protection

The seven sections of `reopen_receipt_contract.required` are a price-agnostic
answer sheet for the existing owner. Each actual value is `null`, not zero:

1. Exact job revision, objective acceptance/rejection, limited rework and dispute terms.
2. Attributable operator authorization: task, amount, asset/currency, network or
   ordinary payment method, payer/source, cap and expiry.
3. Exclusive, verifiable reward reservation and current capacity after prior obligations.
4. Eligible worker's voluntary assent to scope, pay, deadline, effort and remedies.
5. All worker cash costs and setup/active/rework time; all owner inference, gas,
   fees, support, subscriptions/setup, rework, pay and subsidy source.
6. Account-specific lawful spendable payout: currency/FX, minimum, fees, holds,
   timing and cash accessibility. Sensitive account/address evidence stays in
   authorized custody; only a safe reference belongs in GitHub.
7. Conservative incremental net benefit, foregone earnings, fair-pay/effort
   bound, displacement/harms and credible no-assistance comparison.

A reservation alone does not pass; an arithmetic quote is not earned income.
Controller runtime cannot substitute for a worker's effort or country eligibility.

signup, outreach, payments, credit, guarantees or publication of private data.
