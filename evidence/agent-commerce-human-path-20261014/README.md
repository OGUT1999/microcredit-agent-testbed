## Where this fits

Offer a narrow trust service around useful work bought by an independently controlled customer. Start with reproducible code review or integration QA, which the existing agents can deliver using current resources. Sell a named deliverable, acceptance evidence and reliable settlement; do not sell a generic agent marketplace or require buyers to adopt our contract before proving demand.

A functioning steady state would have independent buyers repeatedly ordering useful work, new providers getting their first accepted payment, and successful providers returning for further orders. Buyers fund wages. Any guarantee pool has explicit capital providers and bounded losses; subsidies and operator-funded purchases remain separately visible. A smart contract can bind a job, price, deadline, acceptance/dispute authority, reserve exposure and settlement receipt. It cannot establish real usefulness, independent control, an honest evaluator or a human's usable income by itself.

Proposed evidence gates, not established achievements: first one buyer-funded accepted job; then three jobs with observed full costs; then a 30-day cohort with at least three independently controlled buyers, ten accepted paid jobs and two repeat purchases. Observe margin, acquisition/support effort, disputes and reserve drawdown throughout. Extend the observation period before calling demand or reserve performance steady. A few low-loss jobs do not estimate tail risk.

## Competing hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Risk and beneficiary | Cheapest discriminating buyer fact | Simpler comparison / stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer performance protection | Buyer fears paying a new agent for unusable work; verified failure triggers a bounded refund or replacement | Buyer has a real priced job but declines this provider specifically because performance assurance is absent | Acceptance-based escrow, a small paid milestone or verifiable sample may solve it without pooled insurance. Stop if buyer would hire anyway or only wants a subsidy. |
| Worker nonpayment protection | Provider fears client default after accepted useful delivery; reserve pays a bounded earned wage | Provider would take a specific funded job except for uncovered client default risk | Fully funded escrow or prepayment normally removes this risk more directly. Do not relabel an acceptance dispute as client default. |
| Loan / input advance | Provider lacks a mandatory pre-delivery input despite positive full-cost margin | Exact input quote, actual liquidity shortfall and an assigned funded job | Existing resources, buyer allowance, prepayment or direct funding first. An unfunded job and a lack of reputation are not loan demand. |

Test both guarantee hypotheses in buyer/provider conversations without issuing a guarantee. A paid job that needs no guarantee is a valid commercial result and negative evidence for financing necessity.

## Economics and staged route

For each job record customer payment actually collected minus compute/API, gas/platform/FX/payout fees, support and dispute costs, replacement work and human labor opportunity cost. Show cash margin and full economic margin separately; prepaid operator compute and donated agent time are subsidies, not zero-cost inputs. Count no pass-through transfers as revenue twice.

For a guarantee with expected claim probability p, exposure G, per-job administration c and capital/risk charge k, unconditional collected fee f must exceed pG+c+k for a positive expected contribution before other costs. The worker-replenishment candidate is different: expected reserve change is (1-p)qr-pG-c, where q is collection conditional on buyer payment and r the capped replenishment. Neither expected-value formula establishes finite-pool safety. Do not fit p from a handful of internal jobs. Existing reserve/consent/exposure restrictions remain; no live issuance follows from this brief.

1. Now: qualify one original buyer need and agree a small useful deliverable; no new infrastructure. Hermes obtains the actual response and terms; Codex scopes delivery; Claude identifies only the blocker that prevents this transaction.
2. First paid job: execute only after assignment/authority and costs are clear, record exact delivered artifact, buyer acceptance and settlement. Document what risk the buyer/provider actually raised. If prepayment/escrow suffices, use it subject to existing authority rather than force a guarantee.
3. Repetition: offer the same useful service to repeat and independent buyers, measuring unsubsidized contribution margin and acquisition effort. A second order from the same buyer tests retention; it is not a second independent buyer.
4. Guarantee experiment: only after authorization and priced uncovered risk, test whether bounded protection induces an additional purchase or first-provider hire relative to the simpler alternative. Freeze acceptance and dispute terms before work. Stop if it only shifts payments within common control, loses money without a credible learning objective, or fails to cause useful additional work.
5. Steady state: exposure limits, transparent reserve accounting, repeat buyers and providers, and payment/dispute records support a small market. Broader scale requires observed demand and losses, not extrapolation from testnet or a subsidized launch.

## Concurrent route to humans in need

Do the design and buyer-demand research now; do not wait to declare an agent economy finished. Two routes need separate tests: (A) a human controls an agent-assisted service business and retains net customer earnings; (B) existing agent buyers purchase complementary human work such as local-language QA, accessibility/usability checks or permitted local observations. A machine-only Python review may not supply a defensible human role. Hardware ownership is not evidence of poverty or eligibility.

First establish a buyer's actual willingness to pay for that human contribution. Before any recruitment or work, require a willing eligible adult, informed scope/acceptance, privacy-safe participation, accessible tools, a lawful usable payout rail and a positive conservative net-pay estimate. No unapproved recruitment of vulnerable people is authorized here. Do not charge the worker for access or manufacture a debt to enter the experiment.

Measure earnings received and usable locally minus data/device/electricity costs, payout/FX fees, worker contributions, time and foregone alternative earnings. Record whether a human controls the proceeds and who paid for training and agent compute. Test withdrawal/spendability with appropriate later authority; an agent wallet balance is insufficient. A guarantee payout replaces a failed wage and is not automatically additional income caused by the project.

For a consented later pilot, compare the agent-assisted paid route with the person's available alternative using matched opportunities or an appropriate predeclared comparison. Measure assignment, acceptance, paid outcome, effective hourly net earnings, repeat work, displacement and harms. Stop if the buyer can automate the task without the person, the human merely provides identity/payment access, fees or unpaid search/training erase the gain, payment is unusable, or the work displaces comparable earnings without improvement. No present evidence establishes a household-benefit multiplier.
