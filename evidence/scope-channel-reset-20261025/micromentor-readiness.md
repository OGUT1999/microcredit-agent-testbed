## Disposition and constructive alternative

A new account or a general user approval would not by itself establish either. No new approval question is justified for this unqualified role.

Better alternative: support a qualified, consenting human mentor on one defined preparation task while that person retains judgment and their genuine relationship. This is not a human proxy for publishing prohibited agent content. It still requires the platform's applicable AI/data rules, consent and an actual task; none has been established. Stop if errors, privacy burden or review time erase any saving. Passing such a rehearsal would not establish entrepreneur earnings; later benefit assessment would need actual consent, task completion and net business/household outcomes. No thresholds, participants, launch dates or paid recruitment are invented here.
